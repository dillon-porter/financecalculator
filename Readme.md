# Voltage Loan Calculator

A custom WordPress financing calculator plugin built for **Voltage Powersports** to provide customers with transparent vehicle financing estimates.

This project demonstrates modern WordPress plugin development, responsive UI design, JavaScript financial calculations, and user experience improvements focused on accessibility and pricing transparency.

---

## Overview

The Voltage Loan Calculator allows customers to estimate financing payments for powersports vehicles while providing a clear breakdown of pricing, taxes, fees, and financed amounts.

The calculator was designed with a strong emphasis on **clarity and usability**, ensuring customers of all ages can easily understand how their estimated payment is calculated.

## Features

### Vehicle Selection

* Choose from multiple powersports models
* Dynamic pricing based on selected vehicle
* Easy-to-use dropdown interface

---

### Financing Calculator

Calculate estimated payments based on:

* Financing term (12–72 months)
* Annual interest rate
* Cash down payment
* Payment frequency

  * Monthly
  * Bi-weekly
  * Weekly

---

### Transparent Pricing Breakdown

The calculator provides a detailed cost summary including:

* Vehicle price
* Assembly & Destination Fee
* Subtotal before tax
* HST calculation
* Total purchase price
* Cash down payment
* Amount financed

This approach eliminates hidden fees and helps customers understand exactly how their financing estimate is calculated.

1. Select a vehicle
2. Choose province/tax rate

6. Select payment frequency
7. Generate an estimated payment
8. Review the complete financing breakdown

## Focus on Transparency

One of the primary design goals of this project was improving customer trust.

Rather than displaying only a payment amount, the calculator clearly itemizes:

* Vehicle cost
* Assembly & Destination Fee
* Taxes
* Down payment
* Amount financed

allowing customers to understand every component of their financing estimate.

---

## WordPress Integration

The calculator is delivered as a lightweight WordPress plugin using a shortcode.

```text
[voltage_loan_calculator]
```

making it easy to embed into any page without modifying theme files.
